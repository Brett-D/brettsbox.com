A 2007 Roomba that maps the house by itself.

A 2007-era Roomba 550 with a Jetson Orin Nano and a stereo depth camera on its back. It builds a map of the house with visual SLAM, plans its own routes with Nav2 and explores rooms it has never seen.

// Build log

Fresh off the bench

All 8 entries
  1. #08 15 November 2026 · 4 min From Python to Rust: same robot, a fraction of the CPU Ten of the robot's ROS 2 nodes now have Rust versions, each checked against the Python original output for output. The five busiest went from about 1.3 CPU cores to under a fifth of one.
  2. #07 14 November 2026 · 4 min Basalt vs cuVSLAM, round one: CPU or GPU? The robot can work out how it's moving with Basalt on the CPU or NVIDIA cuVSLAM on the GPU. The GPU holds still better; Basalt stays on track better once it moves. Here's the scorecard so far.
  3. #06 14 November 2026 · 3 min Moving the brains to the GPU: cuVSLAM, nvblox and sensor fusion NVIDIA cuVSLAM for visual odometry, an EKF that fuses calibrated wheels, gyro and vision, and nvblox for obstacle mapping, all on the Jetson's GPU. Plus a frozen node, a smeared costmap and a new bump reflex.
