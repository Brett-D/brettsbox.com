  2. LOG-062026-11-143 min Moving the brains to the GPU: cuVSLAM, nvblox and sensor fusion NVIDIA cuVSLAM for visual odometry, an EKF that fuses calibrated wheels, gyro and vision, and nvblox for obstacle mapping, all on the Jetson's GPU. Plus a frozen node, a smeared costmap and a new bump reflex.
